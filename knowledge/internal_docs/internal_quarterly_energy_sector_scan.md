# Quarterly Energy Sector Scan - Internal Notes
Prepared by: Research & Sourcing team
Coverage: Cross-sector scan, this past quarter
Status: Internal working notes - not a formal investment memo

## Purpose of this document

This is our quarterly cross-sector scan, meant to flag emerging patterns across
the advanced energy space broadly before any single sub-segment gets a formal
diligence memo. It draws on inbound deal flow, LP conversations, and informal
market chatter - treat confidence accordingly, this is directional, not verified.

## Cross-cutting observations this quarter

- Inbound deal flow tagged "advanced nuclear" (SMR + MSR combined) was up
  noticeably quarter-over-quarter, which our sourcing team attributes largely to
  the corporate data-center offtake narrative rather than a change in the
  underlying technology landscape.
- Inbound deal flow tagged "enhanced geothermal" was roughly flat quarter-over-
  quarter, though the average stage of inbound has shifted later (fewer seed-stage
  intros, more Series B+), which may indicate the sub-segment is maturing past its
  earliest, most speculative phase.
- Wave/marine energy inbound remains minimal - fewer than a handful of intros this
  quarter - consistent with our internal view that it remains one of the least
  mature sub-segments in advanced energy generation.

## LP and co-investor sentiment

Multiple LPs have specifically asked about our exposure (or lack thereof) to
"firm, dispatchable clean power" as a category spanning both advanced nuclear and
enhanced geothermal, framed explicitly as a hedge against intermittent renewables'
grid integration challenges. This framing - dispatchability as the unifying thesis
across sub-segments, rather than treating each technology as a separate bet - is
relatively new in how LPs are talking to us about this space, and worth carrying
into any sector briefing we produce.

## Caution on internal recency bias

Our own inbound deal flow patterns above are a lagging and self-selected signal -
they reflect who is choosing to pitch us, not the full external market. Any
internal-only view of "what's changing in advanced energy" should be checked
against external, published market and funding data before being treated as
representative of the sector as a whole.
